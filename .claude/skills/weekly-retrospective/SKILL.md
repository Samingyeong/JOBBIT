# JOBBIT Weekly Retrospective

Use only existing checked task state and user-supplied evidence.

1. Calculate completed/total and hours actual/planned for the specified week.
2. Split summary by planning/operations, frontend, and AI Native.
3. Distinguish checkmarks from verified results and state what remains unproven.
4. Identify at most two blockers; propose at most three prioritized actions.
5. Output factual recap + changed assumption + proposed next week's tasks.
6. Never silently change dates, criteria, or marks.
